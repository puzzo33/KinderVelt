Our Programs

Every program is delivered free of charge by licensed therapists and trained volunteers.

Art Therapy

Creative expression helps children process trauma and rebuild confidence.

Fairy-Tale Therapy

Storytelling gives children a safe way to express fears and find hope.

Animal-Assisted Therapy

Rafa the Giraffe helps children build trust and emotional resilience through play.

Hibuki Therapy

Therapeutic hugging dolls offer comfort and a sense of safety to young children.

Sensory Play

Hands-on activities reduce stress and support healthy emotional development.

Friday Resource Nights

Monthly lectures help parents and caregivers support children's mental health.

Our Mission

Friends of Kinder Velt USA Inc. was established in 2024 to support children impacted by the psychological trauma of war in Ukraine. We partner with local therapists, psychologists, and volunteers to provide free, age-appropriate mental health care — so every child has the chance to heal and thrive.

As a registered 501(c)(3) organization, every contribution is tax-deductible and goes directly toward therapy programs, materials, and professional services.
